Wallet privacy

Wallet has no account of its own and no server. What you enter is kept on your phone, in the app or in your browser. The person who made Wallet cannot see it.

Google Drive copy (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google, Wallet keeps one file with your entries in a hidden app folder of your own Google Drive, so a new phone gets them back. Wallet can read and write only that one file — nothing else in your Drive. Turn it off any time in Settings; to delete the copy, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Wallet → Delete hidden app data.

Photos

Bill photos stay on your phone and are not part of the Drive copy.

Only if you add your own Gemini key and choose to scan a photo, that one photo is sent to Google's Gemini service to read it. On Google's free plan, Google may use it to improve its products.

Backup file

A backup file is one you save yourself. Wallet does not upload it.

No tracking

No ads, no analytics, no cookies for tracking.
